The Barista Knows Something

Try a new coffee spot and ask what regulars secretly love.

How to do it

  1. Pick a cafe you have never entered.
  2. Ask the barista for their pick.
  3. Order it, no edits.

The Three-Bite Field Guide

Try one unfamiliar dish slowly enough to notice what the first bite misses.

How to do it

  1. Choose a dish or snack you have never tried.
  2. Take three deliberate bites: first impression, flavor and texture, then a customary condiment or pairing if one is offered.
  3. Write a five-word field note and answer: would you cross town for it?

Today’s Special: Haiku

A haiku using only words found on a restaurant menu.

How to do it

  1. Open any menu, physical or online.
  2. 5-7-5. Menu words only.
  3. Bonus: read it aloud to your table.
