Front-Row Seats to the Sunset

Find the best nearby viewpoint. Same sun, different story.

How to do it

  1. Pick the best hill or viewpoint near you.

Three Stops, Zero Spoilers

Each friend picks one nearby stop without overexplaining.

How to do it

  1. Invite 2-5 friends.
  2. Each person chooses one stop.
  3. No ratings or review-checking until after.
