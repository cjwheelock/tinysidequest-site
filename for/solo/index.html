Table for One, On Purpose

Take yourself somewhere casual and let the scene unfold.

How to do it

  1. Choose a casual cafe, counter, or restaurant you actually want to visit.
  2. Keep your phone away until after you order and notice three details in the room.
  3. Name the scene before you leave.

The Hero Shot

Turn a completely ordinary backdrop into your film poster.

How to do it

  1. Choose a safe, permitted, wonderfully mundane backdrop.
  2. Set a self-timer and take one fully committed hero portrait.
  3. Give the imaginary movie a title.

Place your phone on a stable surface, stay out of traffic and doorways, and photograph only where it is permitted.

The One-Hour Montage

Give the dormant skill or project its training sequence.

How to do it

  1. Choose one harmless skill or project you keep meaning to start again.
  2. Do three focused fifteen-minute rounds with a five-minute reset between each.
  3. Capture the before, middle, and after without judging the ending.
