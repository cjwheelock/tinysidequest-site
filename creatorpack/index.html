Not sure what to say?

Pick any of these. You don’t need to answer them all or share anything private.

  • Who are you? What are you known for, and what do you care about?
  • What shaped you? A turning point, a powerful experience, or a small moment that stuck with you.
  • What do you love doing? Tell us a story about something you tried and why you’d do it again.
  • What would you pass on? Something your community should try, and what you’d tell a first-timer.

One good story is enough to start. Think out loud. We’ll help turn it into quests.
