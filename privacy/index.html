YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy

SideQuest is built to help you collect real-life stories without making those stories public by default.

Effective August 17, 2026

Information we collect

We collect the information needed to run SideQuest, keep your progress available, and protect invitations.

  • Account information, such as your email address or Apple sign-in identifier when you choose to connect an account.
  • The email address you provide during a web checkout so Stripe and RevenueCat can process the purchase, send a receipt, and deliver an app unlock link.
  • Quest activity, progress, journal notes, photos you choose to add, reactions, and Together participation.
  • Purchase identifiers, entitlement status, Pack Key balance changes, and pack unlocks. Apple processes payment details for App Store purchases and Stripe processes payment details for web purchases; SideQuest does not receive your full payment-card information.
  • App, device, attribution, and usage information used for reliability, fraud prevention, and product analytics.

How we use information

We use this information to provide and secure the app, sync progress, deliver invited quests, respond to support requests, understand product performance, and comply with legal obligations.

Journal and Together sharing

Journal entries are private by default. When you intentionally share a result inside a Together quest, the people identified in the sharing control before you submit can see the shared result and may react to it. SideQuest does not publish your journal to a public feed.

Service providers

We use providers that process information for us under their own security and privacy commitments, including Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and durable Pack Key records; RevenueCat for purchase validation, entitlement status, web-purchase redemption, and Pack Key events; Stripe for web checkout and payment processing; AppsFlyer for attribution; and Apple for sign-in, distribution, and App Store payment processing.

Advertising attribution and measurement

AppsFlyer may process a device identifier and app interactions to measure how people discover and use SideQuest. Apple classifies the device-identifier portion of this attribution activity as tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. SideQuest does not sell your journal entries, photos, sign-in email, or purchase history.

Retention and deletion

We keep information while your account is active and as needed to provide SideQuest, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. You can request account deletion from your profile. Some minimized records may be retained when required for fraud prevention or legal compliance.

Your choices

You choose whether to add photos, connect an account, join a Together quest, and share a result with the people identified in the sharing control before you submit. Device settings control photo, camera, contact, and tracking permissions. You can delete your account from Profile, where SideQuest explains what will be erased before you confirm.

Children and changes

SideQuest is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will remove it. We may update this policy as the product or law changes and will post the effective date here.
