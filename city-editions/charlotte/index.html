Art, Racing and Outdoors

Explore Charlotte through art, science, racing heritage and open-air adventures.

Ten quests connect Uptown museums with Camp North End, Freedom Park and larger regional outings to Carowinds, the Whitewater Center, the Gardens at Stowe and Charlotte Motor Speedway. Each stop offers an observation or creative activity without requiring a thrill ride or sporting event.
