Historic houses, public learning and park paths

Read reconstructed and historic rooms, collect present-day clues and make from public paths.

Ten missions in three groups of separate city visits. Mission San Luis, Goodwood and Big Bend Farm bring admitted reconstruction, house and farm experiences. The Grove’s guided house visit sits beside public-library wayfinding and a McCord Park writing page. Downtown museums and Cascades afternoons

Connect current galleries, historic rooms and actual public park places across two afternoons.

Two connected five-mission afternoons. The first joins an admitted LeMoyne exhibition, Riley House exterior, free Union Bank Museum, Historic Capitol House chamber and the survey plaza. The second follows the historic barbershop exterior, Electric Building pattern, Centennial Field wall, a short pedestrian-bridge return and a dry Boca Chuba pause.
