What’s in the Valencia City Edition

Citywide10 quests

River to Lagoon

Follow Valencia from its historic centre through the Turia corridor to the coast and Albufera.

Ten independent missions across the old centre, Turia gardens, modern complex, coast and lagoon. Plan separate outings and verify access before travel.

Across Valencia

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Market and MerchantsChoose a stall product and record its stall, name and one supported detail.
  • The River Without WaterFollow a named Turia Garden segment and chart how its bridge crossings connect the former riverbed to street level.
  • Architecture in LightCompare the Hemisfèric’s curve with its reflection from a public path.
Local10 quests

Cabanyal and Marina

Spend one or two afternoons reading Valencia’s former fishing quarter through streets, tiles and the sea edge.

Ten short missions in El Cabanyal, Canyamelar and the Marina. Keep the route local, pause often and use public paths.

Cabanyal, Canyamelar and La Marina

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 2 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Fisher QuarterAt Cabanyal Market, compare a current public food display with the district’s documented fishing history.
  • Your Own Tile SequenceBorrow a visible motif on Carrer de la Reina, then give it a new four-part rhythm on paper.
  • Beach FacilitiesFind one public activity space and one visitor service at Cabanyal beach.
