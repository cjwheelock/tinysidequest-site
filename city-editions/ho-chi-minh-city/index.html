What’s in the Ho Chi Minh City City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Parks and City Life

Explore central museums, Cholon street heritage and separate river and lake outings.

Ten missions cover several parts of urban Ho Chi Minh City: a museum-and-park outing, a separate History Museum and Mai Thi Luu visit, a western Cholon walk, and distinct Thu Thiem and Phu My Hung park visits. Two museum admissions and transport between outings cost extra. Temple missions stay on public pavement.

Local10 quests

Two City Walks

Explore maps, architecture, books, art and the river in one connected downtown area.

Ten missions connect the Palace and Cong Xa Paris area with Ly Tu Trong, Ben Thanh, Pho Duc Chinh, Lam Son, Nguyen Hue and Bach Dang. Three visits require paid admission; public exterior and walking missions require no purchase or worship. The Fine Arts Museum’s current session and visitor rules should be checked at its counter.
