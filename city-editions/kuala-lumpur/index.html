What’s in the Kuala Lumpur City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Across the City

Explore tower structures, museum objects and open landscapes across Kuala Lumpur.

Ten varied missions form six separate outings: KLCC, KL Tower, the National Museum, Perdana with IAMM and the National Mosque, Thean Hou Temple, and the National Art Gallery with Titiwangsa. Four missions require paid attraction admission.

Across Kuala Lumpur

Local10 quests

Squares, Craft and Chinatown

Explore the connected civic district and Chinatown through public spaces, craft and visitor galleries.

Ten missions fit two unhurried afternoons in Kuala Lumpur’s historic core: the civic square and riverwalk, then the pioneers museum, Medan Pasar and Chinatown. The pioneers museum requires paid entry; its current counter tariff must be confirmed.

Civic centre and Chinatown

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 2 afternoons

  • A Street That Changes PaceFollow a real market corridor and map where your pace changes.
