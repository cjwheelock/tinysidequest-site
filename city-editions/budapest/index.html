Umbrellas, Ruins and New Steps

Find another Budapest in the spaces beneath umbrellas, above ruined walls and between a garden’s growing surfaces.

Take three separate outings around Óbuda’s Fő tér, across Margaret Island and through Millenáris in Buda. Ten missions turn sculpture gaps, pond layers, ruined rooms and changed factory forms into collections and designs of your own.

Riverlines and Park Rhythms
