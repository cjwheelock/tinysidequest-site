What’s in the Atlanta City Edition

Citywide10 quests

City in Motion

Connect Atlanta’s public landmarks with art, civil rights history and a wider city view.

Ten quests link downtown attractions, Midtown, Buckhead and the historic east side. Indoor museum visits sit alongside park pauses and public viewpoints.

Across Atlanta

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Three Ways to MoveMake a movement key with a simple mark or a movement word for each animal and its displayed name.
  • One PersonRecord the action, the obstacle it addressed and one source detail that makes the account concrete.
  • Rail to ArtAfter leaving the station, note one cue that helps you find the Woodruff Arts Center area.
Local10 quests

Along the Eastside

Follow a former rail corridor through markets, neighborhood paths and public art.

Ten quests connect Krog Street and Inman Park with Ponce City Market, using the Eastside Trail and short neighborhood detours. Markets can be browsed without buying anything.

Old Fourth Ward and BeltLine Eastside

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Color CollisionMake a small abstract design, or describe one in words, showing what happens when those colors touch.
  • Old and NewConnect the three material details in a tiny diagram or a written note explaining how each supports the market’s current use.
  • A Valley MinuteWrite one sentence describing the shape of this small valley without using the word green.
