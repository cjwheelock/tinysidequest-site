What’s in the San Diego City Edition

Park, Bay & Pacific

Explore San Diego’s landmark park, maritime stories and Pacific coast.

Ten quests span Balboa Park, the Zoo, Old Town, the Midway, Gaslamp Quarter, Cabrillo, Coronado and the northern coast. Build several outings around the highlights that interest you.

Balboa Park, Garden to Gallery

Follow the park’s central paths through living collections, public art and exposition architecture.

Ten quests connect the Botanical Building with Spanish Village, El Prado and the gardens south of the museums. The route uses public exteriors and open gardens, with no museum ticket required.
