What’s in the Honolulu City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Royal City, Ocean City

Connect royal history, living culture and the volcanic shape of Honolulu.

Ten quests range from Pearl Harbor and downtown museums to Lēʻahi and the hills above the city. Several visits require admission; the route is designed for separate days.

Across Honolulu

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • One Life, One StoryRecord that person’s role, one event they experienced and one detail you want to remember.
  • Two ObjectsMake a small relationship diagram: object, connection, object. Use the museum’s names and descriptions.
  • Three FindsNote each name and one visible detail; add a question beside the item you would most like to learn about.
Local10 quests

Waikīkī, One Shore at a Time

Find royal connections and small observations along Waikīkī’s public streets and shoreline.

Ten quests follow Waikīkī from Fort DeRussy to Kapiʻolani Park. Read place names, notice buildings and pause at a cultural site without needing a beach activity or hotel booking.

Waikīkī

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Look OutMake three small sketches or written comparisons showing how these features differ from nearby hotel architecture.
  • Root & RoofArrange them from most irregular to most geometric.
  • Room for a StoryWrite one detail you noticed while slowing down, without leaving an object or making an offering.
