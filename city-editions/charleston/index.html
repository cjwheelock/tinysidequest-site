What’s in the Charleston City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Harbor and Lowcountry

Connect Charleston’s museums and harbor with the landscapes around the city.

Ten quests spread across the peninsula, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, James Island and Johns Island. Learn from museum accounts, trace harbor shapes and make time beneath a live oak.

Across Charleston

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Season in Three ShapesDesign a small seasonal card with those shapes and the date of your visit.
  • Land That RemembersRecord the feature and one documented connection it makes with African or African American history.
  • Harbor from the WaterAfter the return, draw three distance bands and place one observed landmark in each.
Local10 quests

From Market to Battery

Read Charleston’s lower peninsula through markets, facades and small harbor views.

Ten quests form a walk through the market district, French Quarter and streets toward the Battery. The route includes a respectful museum stop and keeps private houses and worship spaces outside the mission.

French Quarter and waterfront

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Craft in Three DetailsRecord those details and one thing the object’s construction made you notice.
  • A Street and a SteepleDraw a three-line composition that leads upward without copying all the architectural detail.
  • An Account to CarryRecord two facts in your own words and the exhibit or source that supported them.
