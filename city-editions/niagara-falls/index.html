Gorge to Lake

Explore U.S. side stories from the Niagara Gorge through Lewiston to Lake Ontario.

Ten quests span museums, gorge overlooks, hydroelectric power, outdoor art, historic fortifications and the river mouth. These are separate outings in Niagara Falls, Lewiston and Youngstown.

Between River & Brink

Explore the connected U.S. side State Park paths from Prospect Point to Goat Island.

Ten distinct park quests follow the American Rapids, falls overlooks, public interpretation and Goat Island paths across one or two afternoons.
