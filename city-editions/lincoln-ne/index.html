Mosaics, collections and open paths

Read civic mosaics and collections, make from garden details and take an easy path return.

Ten separate Lincoln arrivals combine the Capitol Foyer, admitted zoo habitats and quilt galleries, natural-history and mechanical collections, public arts galleries, two small garden creations, a short Rock Island return and an east lake-side pause. Downtown and Haymarket in two afternoons

Connect public collections, street details and railway history across two afternoons.

Two connected Downtown and Haymarket afternoons with measured return walking. Return for current regional art, the former depot, a preserved railway park, an original roofline strip and a short Canopy Street return.
