What’s in the Austin City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Capitol, Springs and Lake

Meet Austin through its state landmarks, art, spring-fed water and lakeside paths.

Ten quests connect the Capitol, Bullock and Blanton museums, Zilker gardens, Barton Springs, Mount Bonnell, the Wildflower Center, South Congress and Lady Bird Lake. Plan separate outings for the farther destinations and use the included alternatives when admission, stairs or outdoor conditions do not suit the day.

South Congress in Small Details

Follow the avenue through music, roadside history and things made by hand.

Ten quests explore South Congress from Music Lane to the shops farther south. Public exteriors, displays and short written observations keep the route open to visitors without a hotel booking or a night out.
