What’s in the Denver City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Art, Nature and Red Rocks

Explore Denver through landmark buildings, museum discoveries and a landscape built for music.

Ten quests connect Union Station and Coors Field with the Capitol, major museums, gardens and Red Rocks. Choose several outings, with time for each destination and transport between neighborhoods.

Across Denver

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 flexible chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Story Behind the StandsCarry away one story from a guide rather than a souvenir.
  • Where Two Views MeetLet a short change in viewpoint reveal a different relationship between city and water.
  • One Relationship, a New CompositionUse an artwork’s visual relationship to begin something original.
Local10 quests

LoDo and Small Discoveries

Find architectural rhythms, neighborhood stories and small creative prompts around Lower Downtown.

Ten quests make a downtown loop from Union Station through Dairy Block, Coors Field, Sakura Square and Larimer Square before returning past MCA Denver. The route uses public sidewalks and open visitor spaces, with no game, hotel stay, museum admission or purchase required.

Lower Downtown and Union Station

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 walking chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Three Materials at the OxfordLook closely at a hotel’s public face.
  • An Alley Color MapMake an original record of color relationships in a small space.
  • A Community Story to CarryLearn a place’s story from the people who steward it.
