    Explore Chicago with SideQuest

    Big skyline.
    Small discoveries.

    Chicago rewards a second look. A tower folds into Cloud Gate. A glass dome turns a ceiling into a sky. Along the Riverwalk, each stretch gives you a different reason to stop.

    SideQuest gives your curiosity a starting point: a detail to find, an optional riddle to solve, or a small creative challenge to make the place your own.

    Skyline & Lake

    Look across the city from Skydeck, remix a work at the Art Institute, and compare buildings from an architecture cruise. Continue with Navy Pier, Museum Campus, Griffin Museum of Science and Industry, Lincoln Park Zoo, and a Wrigley Field tour across separate outings.

    Steel & Water

    Explore the Loop, Millennium Park, and the Riverwalk through ten small adventures. Find patterns in the Cultural Center’s glass dome, bend a building in Cloud Gate’s reflection, and read the riverfront through its bridges and public spaces.

    01 / Millennium Park

    Fold the Skyline

    Find a building in Cloud Gate’s reflection, then compare its straight lines with the curves in the mirrored skyline.

    02 / Chicago Cultural Center

    A Sky Made of Glass

    Find a shape and two colors in the Cultural Center’s glass dome. Turn them into your own imagined sunrise.

    20–30 minutesFree entry during public hours

    River Rooms

    Follow the Riverwalk from State Street toward LaSalle and choose the spot you’d return to with twenty unplanned minutes.

    30–40 minutesNo venue purchase needed
