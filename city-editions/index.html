    What will you notice first?

    Start with a reflection, a ceiling full of color, or a new perspective on the river. Each quest gives you a place to begin and something to look for when you get there.

    01 / Millennium Park

    Fold the Skyline

    Find a building reflected in Cloud Gate, then compare its straight edges with their curved reflection. Capture both versions in photos or words, and imagine a new job for the shape you found.

    20–30 minutesFree public artwork

    02 / Chicago Cultural Center

    A Sky Made of Glass

    Look for a repeated shape and two colors in the Tiffany glass dome at Preston Bradley Hall. Turn those colors into an imagined Chicago sunrise, saved as a field note or permitted photo.

    20–30 minutesFree entry during public hours

    03 / Chicago Riverwalk

    River Rooms

    Explore the stretch from State Street toward LaSalle. Read the seating, paths, and water’s edge for clues to how each space works. Pick the spot you would return to with twenty unplanned minutes.

    30–40 minutesNo venue purchase needed

    Why bring SideQuest?

    Give the day a little direction.

    A starting point between plans

    Have a gap before dinner or another museum visit? Choose one short Loop or riverfront quest. You can finish a small adventure without taking on a whole itinerary.

    A reason to look again

    First visit or familiar neighborhood, the prompt changes the experience. Follow a curve, compare a reflection, or make a three-sentence river story from the view in front of you.

    Something to share or keep

    Explore on your own, or compare discoveries with a friend. A photo or field note gives you a record of what caught your attention, beyond the name of the place you visited.

    Make room for your kind of Chicago day.

    A little time: Start with a single Steel & Water quest. Its ten missions stay around the Loop, Millennium Park, and the riverfront; take a chapter at a time or come back another day.

    A longer visit: Spread Skyline & Lake across separate outings. Museum visits, the architecture cruise, and the Wrigley Field tour need their own time and, where required, separate tickets. Quest times exclude travel and queues.

    Before you go: Check current opening hours, access, weather, and booking requirements. The app’s quests include alternatives for changes in access, energy, or comfort.

    Choose your own order and pace. Admission, food, and transport are separate.
