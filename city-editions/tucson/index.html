What’s in the Tucson City Edition

Citywide13 quests

Desert to Sky

Meet Tucson from the Sonoran desert floor to the forests of a sky island.

Thirteen car-friendly missions through saguaro country, canyon water, living food history, desert-built art, aviation, adobe neighborhoods, and the mountain above the basin.

Across Tucson

  • Car recommended
  • Gold at 10

A few quests from this pack

  • The Saguaro Family AlbumTurn three wildly different cactus silhouettes into a desert cast of characters.
  • Let the Canyon SpeakBuild a three-part sensory map of a desert canyon shaped by rare water.
  • The White Dove of the DesertFind the white landmark that still serves the community it was built to serve.
Local12 quests

Streetcar & Side Streets

Meet Tucson block by block without building the day around a car.

Twelve walk-and-streetcar missions through the University of Arizona, Fourth Avenue, downtown, Barrio Viejo, Mercado, and the living agricultural history west of the city core.

Across Tucson

  • Walk + streetcar
  • Two half-days

A few quests from this pack

  • The Avenue Born from an UnderpassRead one Tucson street through its older bones, streetcar rails, and present-day personality.
  • The 23-Stop GalleryTurn Tucson's streetcar stations into one moving public-art exhibition.
  • Cross the CreatureFind the Tucson bridge that turns a practical crossing into a giant desert animal.
