Collections, gardens and open paths

Look closely at city collections, compare garden forms and make original records of parks and historic architecture.

Ten Reno missions span two museums and an automobile collection, a library, lakeside and garden visits, a former school exterior, a short park-path return and a nature-area pause. Paid entry and opening windows vary; plan separate arrivals across the city.

Two downtown afternoons

Connect downtown landmarks, public art, riverfront paths and an indoor garden through two walking afternoons.
