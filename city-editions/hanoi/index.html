What’s in the Hanoi City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Heritage and Lakes

Explore Hanoi through scholarly courtyards, museum evidence, public facades and two different lakes.

Ten missions form west-central paid visits, two central heritage outings, a separate western Ethnology visit and a West Lake pause. The History Museum visit is at 216 Tran Quang Khai. Ordinary tickets and actual vehicle journeys are part of the broader outings; the exterior and shore missions use public space.

Across Hanoi

  • 3 optional riddles
  • Broader outings

A few quests from this pack

  • Borrow a LayoutTurn one gallery observation into an original arrangement.
  • Two Witnesses of TimeCompare a standing monument with evidence beneath it.
  • One Unhurried Lake ViewPause beside West Lake without adding another destination.
Local10 quests

Old Quarter Work and Memory

Explore a connected Old Quarter area through household space, craft, goods, painted memory and public garden time.

Ten missions fit two planned afternoons around Hang Bac, Ma May, Ta Hien, Hang Buom, O Quan Chuong, Cau Dong, Hang Ma, Phung Hung and Hang Dau. Three heritage visits are paid; the other missions stay on public streets or a municipal garden. Confirm Heritage House’s ordinary opening session before the first afternoon.

Old Quarter and Hang Dau

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 2 afternoons

A few quests from this pack

  • The Wholesale DisplayRead how goods are arranged to move through a market.
  • Add One MomentAnswer a public mural with an original everyday scene.
