What’s in the Savannah City Edition

Citywide10 quests

City, Marsh and Sea

Travel beyond the squares to rail history, living coastal culture and the islands.

Ten quests connect Savannah’s western downtown with Bonaventure, the marshes, Pin Point and Tybee. The broad route includes places outside the city and works best as several outings.

Across Savannah

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • An Old Wall, a New FrameMake a two-panel drawing: the same edge seen once as old texture and once as clean shape.
  • A River with a ReturnAfter returning, record one change in the city’s outline that you could see from the far bank or boat.
  • A Quiet Path Near the RiverWrite a calm description of those three elements without inventing a story about anyone buried here.
Local10 quests

Squares to the River

Follow a chain of small public spaces from Forsyth Park to the riverfront.

Ten quests use the historic district’s squares, public sidewalks and exterior landmarks. A compact walk combines drawing, small observations and brief place history without needing a house tour.

Northern historic squares and River Street

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • A Square with Four EdgesWrite which edge feels most open and what visible feature gives you that impression.
  • A Square in Five LinesDraw a five-line composition that includes all three, leaving out every unnecessary detail.
  • A Pause Between StreetsWrite one sentence about how the boundary changes the space, without inventing a story about a burial.
