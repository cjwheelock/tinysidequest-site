Clay, Gardens and Coast

Explore Hoi An through distinct places and purposeful observations.

Ten missions form four sensible Hoi An destination outings: western pottery and a separately admitted park, northern gardens and An Bang, eastern palm channels and Cua Dai, and a town morning of archaeological evidence, a cinnamon display and a personal gallery response. Road travel, visitor admission and current conditions belong in the plan.

Through the Ancient Town

Explore Hoi An through distinct places and purposeful observations.

Ten missions connect two Ancient Town afternoons on named streets, using a paid house and bridge crossing, paid museum/courtyard visits and ordinary public gateway, market and square observations. Shared visitor windows, monthly museum closures and real queues shape the order.
