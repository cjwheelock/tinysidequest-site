What’s in the Da Nang City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Stone, Art and Sea

Explore a market and two museums, then make separate Marble Mountains and Son Tra coastal outings.

Ten missions cover urban and coastal Da Nang in three sensible outings. Paid Fine Arts, Cham Museum and Thuy Son visits sit alongside ordinary market, public-pavement and outdoor visitor stops. Arranged road transfers cost extra. No Hoi An, Ba Na, forest trek or former Quang Nam destination is included.

Along the Han

Explore the Han River’s western city edge and a connected cross-river afternoon.

Ten missions form two daylight afternoons around the Han: a public government-building view, the paid new museum, bridge and market observations, and a southern river circuit. The second afternoon uses the Dragon Bridge sidewalk and Nguyen Van Troi pedestrian crossing, with steps and busy street approaches. Carp observation uses a currently open ordinary admitted landward landscaped visitor plaza; Love Bridge observation uses its currently open ordinary admitted upper visitor walk and viewing bays.
