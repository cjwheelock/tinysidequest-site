Curves and Open Sky

Read carved stone, collect a tiled wave and let an eastern waterfront view shift.

Ten outdoor missions connect broad shapes with small discoveries in three separate outings: Belém, Estrela and Amoreiras, and Parque das Nações. Compose a stone silhouette, take an iron-and-tree pause and solve three optional exterior riddles.
