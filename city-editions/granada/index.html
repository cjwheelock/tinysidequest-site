What’s in the Granada City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Water & Wonder

Explore Granada through palace water, market choices, a park poem and a small science experiment.

Ten quests across the Alhambra and Albaicín, the historic center and the western park and science district. Three separate outings combine paid monument visits with free outdoor activities and optional riddles.

Across Granada

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Three Jobs for WaterFind water moving, reflecting and helping a garden grow during your Alhambra visit.
  • A Meal Without a BagCompose an imaginary meal from three things offered at the market.
  • Guess, Try, SeeMake two predictions, try two exhibits and let the results surprise you.
Local10 quests

Realejo Rooms

Spend two afternoons among Realejo squares, old rooms and connected garden slopes.

Ten quests in the lower Realejo and the streets above it: discover a carved heart, sort a building’s layers, invent a street score and compare garden spaces. The upper afternoon includes steep streets and steps; the lower route includes an optional paid interior.

Realejo

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 2 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Room LayersSeparate a surviving room, a later building and a garden story.
  • A Score for This StreetMake a tiny score from a repeated sound, a pause and a change.
  • Garden MoodsFind two garden spaces and decide which details make them feel different.
