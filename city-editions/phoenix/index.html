Desert and Discovery

Explore Phoenix through desert forms, museum discoveries and wide views.

Ten quests span central Phoenix, Papago Park, the northern museums and nearby Scottsdale. Plan several outings with separate rides between districts; each quest includes an alternative when admission, heat or access does not suit your day.
