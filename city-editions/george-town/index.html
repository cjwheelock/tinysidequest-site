What’s in the George Town City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Gardens, Coast and Everyday Objects

Explore George Town’s public coast, gardens and everyday heritage, with a separate Air Itam and Penang Hill day trip.

Ten missions form several sensible outings: the Farquhar museum and civic shore, P. Ramlee’s birthplace, Pulau Tikus and Gurney, the gardens, and a separate Kek Lok Si / Penang Hill trip. The state tourism bureau treats the last two attractions as outside George Town. Museum entry and the funicular are paid; general gardens and the scoped public paths are free.

Living Heritage Streets

Explore the connected heritage core through public street views, admitted clan and Mansion visits, and a respectful quay pause.

Ten missions fit two unhurried afternoons among the Street of Harmony, Church Street, Armenian Street and Weld Quay. The Mansion, Cheah Interpretation Centre and Khoo Kongsi visits require paid admission. Religious exteriors and the final Chew frontage use ordinary public paths; no boardwalk or home entry is needed.
