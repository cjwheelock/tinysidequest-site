What’s in the Fresno City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Gardens, Art and County Stories

Explore Fresno through admitted gardens, museums, zoo spaces and public street architecture.

Ten missions across three flexible outings connect northwest gardens, central art and theatre stops, and southeast history and science. Paid entries, booked openings and travel between separate districts are part of the plan.

Across Fresno

Local10 quests

Civic Corners and Fulton Art

Explore the connected downtown streets through public exterior landmarks, art, movement and a park pause.

Ten missions fit two unhurried downtown afternoons: civic exteriors and north Fulton, then Mariposa Plaza, the southern art block and Courthouse Park. The activities use ordinary open public spaces without theatre tickets or private building entry.
