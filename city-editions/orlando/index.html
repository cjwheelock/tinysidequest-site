What’s in the Orlando City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Theme Parks and Green Escapes

Explore Orlando’s theme-park landmarks, science, wildlife and gardens across separate outings.

Ten quests take you from Walt Disney World and Universal Orlando to International Drive, Gatorland, Leu Gardens, Orlando Science Center and nearby Winter Park. Activities fit into visits you choose; park tickets, transport and attraction admission are separate.

Across Orlando

  • 3 optional riddles

Downtown to Lake Eola

Follow downtown details from historic storefronts to a lakeside pause.

Ten quests connect Church Street architecture, CityArts, the History Center frontage and the public library with Lake Eola. Explore on foot, with no theme-park ticket, museum admission or purchase required.
