Columns, Collections and Water

Find copper dragons, a suspended sphere and a half-moon pond, then make the city your own observation notebook.

Ten quests span Taipei's western streets, eastern neighbourhoods and northern visits. Watch night-market work, study habitat design, borrow a museum object's detail and take a short walk through a flood-control park. Three optional riddles reveal their answers whenever you choose.

Dadaocheng in Two Afternoons

Follow letters, fabric and tea work from Beimen's museums to the Tamsui River.

Ten connected quests use two afternoon walks through Beimen and Dadaocheng. Make a pocket route card, give cloth patterns a purpose, turn tea production into a short story and pause at the wharf. Three optional riddles use real objects, buildings and exhibition interpretation, with immediate reveals available.
