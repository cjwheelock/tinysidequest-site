What’s in the Edinburgh City Edition

Citywide10 quests

Rock, Hill and Shore

Read Edinburgh from its castle rock and old volcano to a river path and the sea.

Ten quests across the Old Town, Holyrood Park, Portobello’s shore, the Water of Leith and the Botanic Garden. Count, sketch, walk and pause at your own pace over three outings.

Across Edinburgh

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 3 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Three Ages on the RockFind three castle features from three different centuries and put them in time order.
  • The Outline of an Old VolcanoDraw the skyline of Arthur’s Seat and the crags from the loch path, then mark the chapel ruin.
  • The Garden Above the SkylineFind three garden places and name one city landmark you can pick out from the high viewpoint.
Local10 quests

Leith and Newhaven

Spend two afternoons in Edinburgh’s port: its links, quays, docks and fishing harbour.

Ten quests in Leith and Newhaven: a park with a disputed siege story, the quayside at The Shore, a short tram ride to the harbour and a royal yacht. Everything is within walking distance or four tram stops.

Leith and Newhaven

  • 3 optional riddles
  • 2 chapters

A few quests from this pack

  • Two Mounds, Two StoriesFind both grassy mounds, read their plinths, then check the official record for the doubt.
  • Four Stops to the HarbourRide from Foot of the Walk to Newhaven and note how the view changes from street to docks and harbour.
  • Two Lights, One HarbourDraw Newhaven’s two harbour lights side by side and label how they differ.
