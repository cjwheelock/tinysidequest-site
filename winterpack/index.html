Gingerbread Zoning Violation

Build a gingerbread house with one completely unnecessary feature.

How to do it

  1. Build a gingerbread house from a kit or from scratch. Let the walls set before adding the roof.
  2. Add one feature no house needs: a rooftop hot tub, a dramatic balcony, or a garage for a single gummy bear.
  3. Write the property a real-estate listing worthy of its deeply questionable construction. Include an asking price and make your unnecessary feature the main selling point.

Plan on 1–2 hours with a kit. Allow extra time for baking and cooling if you’re building from scratch, and follow your icing’s setting instructions.

Hot Chocolate, Strong Opinions

Three hot chocolates, one house specialty.

How to do it

  1. Make three small hot chocolates at home, varying the chocolate, spice, or topping. Or try one at each of three cafés.
  2. Taste the homemade versions side by side, or jot down what stands out at each café. Crown a winner.
  3. Give the winner a name that sounds like it costs twice as much.

The Neighborhood Lighting Committee

Walk after dark and judge the winter lights with unofficial authority.

How to do it

  1. Walk a familiar, well-lit route after dark, on your own or with a friend.
  2. Find three winter light displays you can see from the sidewalk.
  3. Give each display an award. Try Best Tiny Balcony, Most Committed Shrub, or Outstanding Use of One Extension Cord.
  4. Keep the verdicts to yourself or share them only with the friend who came along.

Bambi on Ice

Complete one lap of a rink at your own pace.

How to do it

  1. Find a rink with a public skating session. Indoor rinks count, and rental skates are normal.
  2. Take one lap at your own pace. The railing has helped many promising careers.
  3. If you feel comfortable, try one new skill, like a controlled stop.

A Very Small Summer

Visit a greenhouse while it’s cold outside.

How to do it

  1. Visit a public greenhouse, conservatory, or indoor botanical garden.
  2. Find the strangest plant in the room and make a quick sketch of it.
  3. Read the label or look up its name. You’ve been introduced now.

The Cabin Fever Cup

Host a small game tournament with unreasonable stakes.

How to do it

  1. Invite a few people. Ask everyone to bring or teach a game they played as a kid.
  2. Make a trophy out of whatever’s around and decide how to score the tournament.
  3. Play enough games for every person to try at least one they’ve never played.
  4. Award the trophy. The winner is expected to defend it with a straight face.

Hibernation Headquarters

Build a blanket fort big enough to actually sit in.

How to do it

  1. Drape blankets over chairs and pull some pillows inside. A blanket over your head is a hat.
  2. Bring in a warm drink and sit up. If you can’t sit upright, raise the roof.
  3. Watch a movie you loved as a kid from inside your fort.

Power Outage, On Purpose

One evening by candlelight. No screens allowed.

How to do it

  1. Turn off the room lights, put your phone in a drawer, and light a few candles.
  2. Play cards or a board game, or read a book.
  3. Before bed, write down what the evening sounded like.

Battery candles count. Leave the electricity on.

Forecast: Paper

Six paper snowflakes, one winter window.

How to do it

  1. Fold and cut six paper snowflakes, each with a different pattern. Scrap paper is welcome.
  2. Unfold them. If one turns out to be mostly hole, it gets a spot too.
  3. Arrange all six in a window to make a winter scene.

The Tradition Heist

Ask three people about a winter tradition, then steal one.

How to do it

  1. Ask three people about a winter tradition in their family.
  2. Pick your favorite and try it yourself this week.
  3. Report back to the person you stole it from. Tell them how it went.

Doorstep Elf Protocol

Leave a small anonymous treat for someone who won’t see it coming.

How to do it

  1. Pick someone you know: a neighbor, a coworker, or your regular barista.
  2. Leave a small treat and an unsigned note somewhere they’ll find it.
  3. Tell no one.

Choose a sealed treat with the ingredients clearly labeled.

The Market Stall Jury

Find the strangest handmade thing at a winter market.

How to do it

  1. Do one full lap of a winter market before you pick anything.
  2. Go back to the strangest handmade thing you saw. Ask the maker how it came to exist.
  3. Buy something small from their stall as a thank-you.
