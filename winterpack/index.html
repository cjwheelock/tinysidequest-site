Gingerbread Zoning Violation

Build a gingerbread house with one completely unnecessary feature.

How to do it

  1. Build a gingerbread house from a kit or from scratch. Let the walls set before adding the roof.
  2. Add one feature no house needs: a rooftop hot tub, a dramatic balcony, or a garage for a single gummy bear.
  3. Write the property a real-estate listing worthy of its deeply questionable construction. Include an asking price and make your unnecessary feature the main selling point.

Plan on 1–2 hours with a kit. Allow extra time for baking and cooling if you’re building from scratch, and follow your icing’s setting instructions.

The Neighborhood Lighting Committee

Walk after dark and judge the winter lights with unofficial authority.

How to do it

  1. Walk a familiar, well-lit route after dark, on your own or with a friend.
  2. Find three winter light displays you can see from the sidewalk.
  3. Give each display an award. Try Best Tiny Balcony, Most Committed Shrub, or Outstanding Use of One Extension Cord.
  4. Keep the verdicts to yourself or share them only with the friend who came along.

Hibernation Headquarters

Build a blanket fort big enough to actually sit in.

How to do it

  1. Drape blankets over chairs and pull some pillows inside. A blanket over your head is a hat.
  2. Bring in a warm drink and sit up. If you can’t sit upright, raise the roof.
  3. Watch a movie you loved as a kid from inside your fort.
